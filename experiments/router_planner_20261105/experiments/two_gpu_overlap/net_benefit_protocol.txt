Two-rank combined-cost protocol review, 2026-11-05

Measured scope: GPU/runtime planner, full expert weight transfer, actual token
dispatch and identity-output combine. No expert GEMM, model forward pass or
backward/optimizer step is represented by this experiment.

Required comparisons (same layer, physical devices, route rows, expert shapes,
precision, load constraints, slot limits and SM budgets):
- native_cold_serial and native_cold_early
- optimized_cold_serial and optimized_cold_early
- native_hot and optimized_hot only with exact same-version slot contents

Each repetition rotates mode order; preserve every per-rank sample and failure.
Take max(rank0, rank1) for each repetition BEFORE computing aggregate statistics.
Do not compare average-rank time, min-rank time, or isolated component sums.
The complete early-copy path must be timed directly: independent-stream kernels
share HBM, SM and NVLink resources, and isolated overlap estimates miss contention.

Cold means copies are issued in this invocation, for both methods. Report each
method's copy count, the DIFFERENCE in copied expert bytes, and distinct remote
(token,destination rank) counts for token payload. The BF16 hidden-vector payload
reduction for dispatch plus rank-reduced combine is 2 * H * 2 * removed_remote.
That expression excludes metadata, router weights, padding, protocol overhead,
and gradients. It is not a substitute for end-to-end latency.

Hot correctness requires preparing the exact requested expert/slot mapping before
EVERY timed hot sample when variants share destination slots. A prior optimized
cold call can otherwise overwrite slots subsequently assumed hot by native.
Preload+verify outside the hot interval, then execute the full planner again in
the measured interval. Record that this is same-version/same-map reuse. Identity
expert outputs alone cannot detect an incorrect replica value, because they do
not read expert weights. Verify all active replica contents separately.
For synthetic weight verification, W1=e%31+1 and W2=e//31+1 jointly encode each
expert uniquely. Two modulo-31 values do not: experts e and e+31 would collide.

Full GPU timing must refresh sampled routes and owners from this iteration's
source shards; fixed random sample IDs can be prepared, but cached sample route
contents are a narrower component experiment. The current benchmark's
res.run(..., local_routes=routes) uses the complete route+owner collective.

The analysis helper tests each optimized cold schedule against BOTH native cold
schedules. Its conservative measured gate requires >=10 paired samples, every
observed paired advantage exceeding a guardband, and a positive paired-bootstrap
95% lower bound for the mean. It evaluates both CUDA and host segment durations.
This is a finite-cohort eligibility rule, not a guarantee about future batches.
If it fails, do not enable the optimization by default or report zero cost for
rejected exploration. Falling back after residency/exchange cannot refund that
already executed GPU work. A real online guard must cover this sunk cost, or use
an independently calibrated pre-planning rule whose own overhead is measured.

For unchanged weights and unchanged mapping, reuse is an analytical what-if:
  average saving(M) = hot_saving + (cold_saving-hot_saving)/M.
Both baseline and candidate get one cold invocation and M-1 hot invocations.
A weight update invalidates reuse. A change of mapping invalidates affected slots.
An inference or microbatch reuse threshold is not a training end-to-end result.
Training also needs replica gradient accumulation/version ordering before the
owner's optimizer update. Weight bytes and full-path time should be reported for
the tested H/I shapes; router traces alone do not establish expert parameter size.

DeepEP v1's deduplicated receive rows need extra permute/GEMM/unpermute for a real
model. Its raw dispatch/combine probe cannot be substituted for MoonEP's expanded
expert layout in a global latency claim. All DeepEP version/backend names and
weight-transport names must remain explicit in the result tables.
